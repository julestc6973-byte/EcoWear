Our stamp

When a piece passes our checks, it gets a small EcoWear stamp linked to its batch. The stamp tells you three things: who made the piece and from what, that we examined it, and that everyone involved is paid as agreed.

Three checks behind every stamp

1. Traceability

The producer, the creator, the workshop, the material, its origin and its batch are identified, with documents to prove it.

2. Quality

We examine a sample: material, seams, finishes, dimensions, comfort and care instructions.

3. Fair pay

Each person's share and our commission are agreed in writing, and we follow the payments after every sale.

Our rule

No file, no sample or no revenue-sharing agreement: no stamp.

After a sale, we track the amounts owed and the amounts paid. If an agreement is not respected, the stamp is suspended until it is.

What our stamp is not

Our stamp describes an internal check carried out by our team. It is not an independent public certification, such as an organic label, and we never present it as one. On the products page, the stamp file of each piece shows exactly what was checked, so that you can decide for yourself.

Check a batch

Type the batch code printed on the label of your piece. Try BR-01 (our bracelet) or TB-01 (our tote).
