Our Story

Ecowear began in Lyon's Gerland district when three former classmates, Camille Laurent, Shamista Mehta, and Shujuan Zhang, started comparing notes on the same frustration from three different angles. Camille had spent years inside ethical sourcing relationships and had seen first-hand how easily a nice story about a supply chain can outrun the facts behind it. Shamista had built detailed spreadsheets of transport distances, emission factors, and labour conditions for dozens of suppliers, data that rarely left her laptop and never reached an actual customer. Shujuan had watched how quickly shoppers learn to distrust a green logo that isn't backed by anything real. Together they realised the problem was never a shortage of sustainable fashion brands, it was that almost nobody outside those brands could verify their claims. So instead of designing or selling clothing themselves, they founded Ecowear as an independent certification body. We audit a brand's textiles and its treatment of the people who made them, and once a supply chain passes every check, we hand that brand an Ecowear certificate it can show its own customers: evidence, not just a promise. That is still our only product: trust that a customer can check for themselves, rather than trust they are simply asked to give.

Our Mission

Make sustainable fashion verifiable: we audit the materials, the wages and the carbon footprint of independent fashion brands, and we connect the brands that pass with customers who want to buy from them with confidence.

Why We Exist

Most shoppers want to buy ethical, sustainable fashion, but very few can confidently identify which brands actually deserve that trust. This gap between what people want and what they can verify is what we call the information gap, and closing it is the entire reason Ecowear exists. Instead of asking customers to simply believe a brand's claims, we give brands the means to prove them — and shoppers the means to check them.

How the Platform Works

  1. Brands apply: an independent brand registers and pays for an audit of one or more product lines.
  2. We audit: our auditors check the material, the wages and the carbon footprint.
  3. Brands sell: certified brands are listed in our directory and sell their garments in our shop.
  4. Customers check and buy: anyone can check a certificate number, or scan a label with our live check, before buying.
