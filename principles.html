Our Principles

Five commitments every certified supply chain is checked against before it earns the Ecowear mark.

  1. Minimise toxic waste and microfibre pollution
  2. Responsible material sourcing
  3. Fair wages and safe working conditions
  4. Circular practices: repair, recycle, upcycle
  5. Full transparency on sourcing and impact

1. Minimise Toxic Waste and Microfibre Pollution

We certify suppliers only once they can demonstrate compliance with strict chemical safety standards, with documented evidence of proper wastewater treatment and chemical handling throughout production.

2. Responsible Material Sourcing

We require certified natural fibres and recycled materials, and a fully documented chain of custody from raw material to finished garment before a certificate is issued.

3. Fair Wages and Safe Working Conditions

From raw material sourcing to retail, every certified supply chain is audited against fair labour standards, with safe working conditions and fair pay as non-negotiable requirements, not aspirations.

4. Circular Practices

We favour brands that design for longevity, repair, and reuse, so that fewer garments end up discarded and more stay in circulation for years.

5. Full Transparency

Every claim behind a certificate — sourcing, materials, environmental impact — is backed by data that customers, regulators, or researchers can access and independently verify. A claim without evidence is not transparency, it is just assertion.
