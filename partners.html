Become a partner

EcoWear connects producers of materials, creators and workshops with customers who want to know where their pieces come from. You stay the seller and ship your own orders. We select the partners, put you in touch and check each piece before it gets our stamp.

Who we work with

Material producers

You collect, recover or make the material: recovered material, reused fabric or documented textile.

  • Documents showing the origin of your material
  • A batch number for each delivery
  • A written agreement on your share

Creators and designers

You design the pieces. We introduce you to producers whose materials we have documented.

  • Your designs and a first sample
  • The name of the workshop that makes your pieces
  • A written agreement with your producer

Workshops

You cut, sew or assemble the pieces. Your seams and finishes are part of our quality check.

  • Your address and who works with you
  • A sample of each new piece
  • Sizes and care instructions

How a partnership works

  1. You send your file: who you are, which material you use, where it comes from and its batch.
  2. We examine a sample: material, seams, finishes, dimensions, comfort and care.
  3. We agree on the money in writing: each person's share and our commission.
  4. Your piece gets the stamp and goes on sale on EcoWear. You stay the seller and ship your orders.
  5. After every sale, we track what is owed and what is paid. If an agreement is not respected, the stamp is suspended.

Our commission

15% per sale

Our commission is 15% of the price of each piece sold. We will negotiate it with our first partners during the pilot.

Example: a €15 bracelet

€2.25 goes to EcoWear and €12.75 to the seller partner, before costs. The seller then shares this amount with the producer, as agreed in writing.
