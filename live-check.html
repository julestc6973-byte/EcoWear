Try the Live Certificate Check

A working prototype of our self-service verification tool. Point a camera at a garment and our machine learning model tells you whether it sees an Ecowear certified seal.

How it works

We trained an image model with Google Teachable Machine on two classes, “certified label” and “no label”. The model runs in your browser: the camera image is never sent to a server.

Using AI Responsibly

  • It can be wrong. The model gives a probability, not a proof. Only the certificate number, checked on our Certified Brands page, proves that a label is genuine.
  • Your privacy. The camera only starts when you click the button, and nothing is recorded.
  • Humans decide. Certificates are issued by our auditors after a full audit, never by the AI.
