Fair-Wage Audit

Proof that the people who made a garment were paid fairly for it — not just a line in a sustainability report.

We review payroll records and interview workers across a brand's supply chain, comparing pay against regional living-wage benchmarks. A certificate is only issued once every workshop in the chain clears the bar.

How We Audit a Workshop

  1. Collect payroll records for every worker across a full pay cycle.
  2. Compare pay against the regional living-wage benchmark for that role.
  3. Interview a sample of workers directly, without supervisors present.
  4. Re-audit every 12 months to confirm standards are maintained, not just met once.

In our last audit cycle, we reviewed workshops across three countries. Any workshop that didn't initially meet the benchmark was given 90 days to correct pay before re-audit, rather than being simply failed and dropped.
