Our pilot

We are starting small: one piece, one batch and twelve weeks to learn. This page shows the order in which we launch our pieces, the steps of the pilot and what we measure.

Launch order

1. Recovered-material bracelet

Kenya · for gift buyers and shops

€15 working price

Before the stamp: link the material to its batch, then validate the sample and the share between the producer and the creator.

2. Reused-textile tote or pouch

Kenya or Tanzania · for students and travelers

€29 tote · €19 pouch

Before the stamp: check the fabric, its origin, the workshop, the seams and the amount owed to each person.

3. Documented-textile T-shirt

Partners being selected · for buyers who want traceability

€35 working price

Before the stamp: check the composition, the place of manufacture, the sizes, the quality and how revenue is shared.

Five steps

  1. Find the people. Identify the producer and the creator of the bracelet.
  2. Check the proof. Review their documents and examine a sample.
  3. Agree on the amounts. Write down each person's share and our commission.
  4. Listen to buyers. Interview 15 buyers about the bracelet, its price and the stamp.
  5. Test for 12 weeks. Sell the bracelet and track quality, sales and payments.

What we measure for 12 weeks

  • Quality: does the piece you receive match the sample we checked?
  • Sales: how many bracelets sell at €15, and to whom?
  • Payments: what is each partner owed, and what has been paid?

At the end of the test, we decide what to change and which piece comes next: the tote or the pouch.
