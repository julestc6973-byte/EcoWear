AI Label Scanner

Not sure whether the label inside a piece is a real EcoWear label? Show it to our scanner. It uses machine learning: a computer program that learned to recognize our label by looking at many examples, instead of following rules written by a person.

How our AI learned

1. Gather

We grouped photos into two classes: EcoWear label (our woven label and hang tag, close up and far away) and No EcoWear label (garments, fabrics and other labels).

2. Train

Google Teachable Machine trained a model on these examples. We then tested it on new photos it had never seen, to check that it gives the right answer.

3. Export

We exported the model and connected it to this page. It runs inside your browser with the TensorFlow.js library.

Using AI responsibly

The AI can be wrong. It gives a probability, not a proof. Only the certificate number proves that a creator is certified: check it on our label page.

Humans stay in the loop. Creators are certified by independent auditors, never by the AI. Your answers above help us add better examples the next time we train the model.

Your privacy is protected. The photo is analyzed on your own device. We do not store it, and we do not use it to train the model without asking you.

Its knowledge is limited. The model learned from a small number of photos, mostly of T-shirts. Blurry, dark or very far photos give less reliable answers.
