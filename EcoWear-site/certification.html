Get certified

EcoWear is made for small independent creators: workshops of one to ten people who design and make their own pieces. Here is how to get our label, what it costs and what it brings you.

Five steps to the label

  1. Test yourself. Answer our self-assessment at the bottom of this page.
  2. Send your file. Create a creator account, then tell us about your materials, suppliers, workshop, packaging and repairs.
  3. Meet the auditor. An independent auditor visits your workshop and scores our six criteria.
  4. Get your certificate. With 20 points out of 30, and at least 2 in every criterion, you receive the label and its certificate number.
  5. Renew every year. A new audit each year keeps the label valid.

What the label brings you

Trust

Print the label on your tags, website and social media, with your certificate number.

A place in our directory

Customers find you by the kind of piece you make, with your scores.

A shop window

Sell your pieces in our shop. You keep shipping them yourself.

Your story

We write about your workshop in our stories and share it with our community.

Fees

€190

once, for your application and your first audit.

€12 per month

for the label if you sell less than €50,000 a year, and €24 per month above. The yearly audit is included.

15% per sale

only on the pieces you sell through our shop.

Self-assessment

For each criterion, choose the answer that describes your workshop today. The value of each answer is its number of points.
