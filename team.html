Our team

EcoWear is run by three people. Each of us looks after one part of the label, so you always know who to ask.

Jules

Criteria and audits

Jules keeps our six criteria up to date, reviews the creators' files and works with the independent auditors.

Josh

Fees, payments and partnerships

Josh sets our fees, follows the payments of the shop and signs our partnerships with auditors.

Joseph

Website, directory and stories

Joseph builds this website, keeps the directory up to date and writes our creator stories.

Our independent auditors

We do not audit the creators ourselves. Our auditors are independent professionals who work neither for EcoWear nor for the creators they check. They score our six criteria and send their report to the creator and to us.
